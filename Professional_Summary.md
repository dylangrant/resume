# Dylan Grant — Professional Summary

Dylan Grant is a frontend-focused engineering leader with 12 years of software development experience, including 5+ years in Engineering Management. Based in Portland, Oregon, he is currently focused exclusively on Engineering Manager roles, having made a deliberate decision in June 2026 to move away from individual contributor positions entirely.

**Career Narrative**

Dylan builds environments where people do the best work of their careers. His approach to engineering leadership centers on creating the conditions, systems, and team culture that let engineers operate at their highest level, rather than managing through process for its own sake. This philosophy has been shaped by direct, hands-on leadership at product-focused companies, paired with ongoing technical depth maintained through active building (not just managing) as the solo founder of his own company.
